# Preventive care: the key habits

Watching your rabbit every day is the best prevention tool you have. Their
appetite, droppings, energy level and cleanliness say a lot about how they feel.

## What to check every day

- **Appetite**: is your rabbit eating its hay as usual?
- **Droppings**: their number and size are a good sign of healthy digestion.
- **Energy**: a dull, hunched rabbit, or one hiding more than usual, deserves
  your attention.
- **Cleanliness**: a soiled rear end can signal a problem.

## Emergency signals

A rabbit that **stops eating** or **stops producing droppings** for a few hours
is an **emergency**. Don't wait: quickly contact a vet who knows rabbits.

## Staying organised

Vaccination and regular weight tracking round out everyday prevention. Keep your
rabbit's health record up to date in the app so nothing slips through: log the
weight, the vaccines and the appointments.
