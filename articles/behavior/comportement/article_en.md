# Understanding your rabbit's behaviour

Your rabbit communicates a lot through **body language**. The ears, the body
position and foot thumping each carry meaning.

## Reading the signals

- **Ears up**: your rabbit is alert and paying attention.
- **Body stretched out and relaxed**: they feel safe.
- **Foot thump**: a sign of worry or displeasure.

## Lowering stress

A calm environment, hiding spots and regular time out of the enclosure all reduce
your rabbit's stress. Respect their pace and **let them come to you** rather than
forcing contact: trust is built over time.

## When to worry

A **sudden change in behaviour** can reveal pain or discomfort. Don't wait to see
a vet if your rabbit withdraws, grinds its teeth loudly or refuses all contact.
