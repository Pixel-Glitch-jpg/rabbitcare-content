# Water and pellets: amounts and good choices

After hay and greens, water and pellets round out your rabbit's diet. Well managed,
they are simple — as long as you avoid a few pitfalls.

## Water: always fresh, always available

- Your rabbit must have **clean water at all times**, refreshed **at least once a
  day** (ideally twice).
- A rabbit drinks on average **around 120 ml per kilo per day**, but this varies: it
  drinks **less** when eating lots of water-rich greens, and **more** in hot
  weather.

### Bowl or bottle?

Many rabbits **prefer a bowl**: they drink in a more natural position and much
faster than from a bottle. A **heavy ceramic bowl** (that won't tip over) is an
excellent choice. If you use a bottle, don't leave it as the **only** water source,
and check every day that the spout isn't blocked.

## Pellets: little, but good quality

Pellets are only a **small supplement** (about **5%** of the diet).

- **Amount**: aim for roughly **15 to 25 g per kilo of rabbit per day**, about **one
  tablespoon** for a medium-sized rabbit. Use the lower end if your rabbit eats lots
  of greens.
- **Don't "top up"** the bowl: too many pellets and your rabbit eats less hay.

### The right type of pellets

Choose **uniform pellets** (all the same), high in fibre. **Avoid "muesli" mixes**
(with flakes, seeds, coloured bits): the rabbit **picks out** only the sugary
pieces, which encourages deficiencies, dental problems and weight gain. Prefer
**timothy-based** pellets for an adult.
