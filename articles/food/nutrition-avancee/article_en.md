# Advanced rabbit nutrition

**Unlimited hay** remains the foundation of your rabbit's diet: it makes up most
of the ration and keeps their teeth naturally worn down.

## Hay first

Offer fresh hay at all times. It is the single most important food: it keeps your
rabbit busy, wears down their constantly growing teeth and supports healthy
digestion.

## Pellets and greens

- **Pellets**: match them to your rabbit's size and limit them in adults to
  avoid weight gain.
- **Greens**: introduce them gradually and rotate well-tolerated plants. The
  **Food** section of the app shows you what is suitable.

## Watch the weight

Weigh your rabbit every week. A **rapid change** — up or down — should alert you
and, if needed, prompt a vet visit.
