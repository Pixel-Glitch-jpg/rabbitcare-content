# Introducing vegetables to a baby rabbit

A young rabbit's digestive system is still **very delicate**. Introducing greens too
early or too fast is one of the main causes of diarrhoea in baby rabbits, a problem
that can turn serious within hours. Here is how to do it gently.

## First things first: milk, hay and pellets

- **Until weaning** (around 6 to 8 weeks), a baby rabbit mainly needs its
  **mother's milk**. No vegetables.
- **From weaning to about 3 months**, the foundation is **unlimited hay** (alfalfa
  hay, which is richer, suits growing youngsters well) together with **pellets made
  for young rabbits** and **fresh water** always available.
- At this stage, **still avoid greens**: the gut is not ready.

## When to start greens?

You can offer the **first vegetables at around 3 months (12 weeks)**, and only if the
youngster is doing well (normal transit, well-formed droppings). There is no rush: it
is better to wait an extra week than to go too fast.

## How to introduce them, step by step

1. **One new vegetable at a time**, in a **very small amount** (about one or two
   small leaves).
2. **Wait 24 to 48 hours** and **check the droppings**.
3. If the droppings stay **well formed**, you can give a little more, then a few days
   later try another vegetable the same way.
4. If the droppings turn **soft** or runny, **stop** that vegetable and go back to hay
   alone. Try again later, in a smaller amount.

Start with **mild, well-tolerated leaves**: a little romaine, carrot tops or a few
herbs (parsley, coriander). At first, avoid vegetables that ferment (cabbage,
broccoli) and sugary vegetables.

## Increase little by little

Over the weeks you can **vary the greens** and **increase the amounts** as the rabbit
grows, gradually reaching an adult's ration (see the article "The right leafy greens
for your rabbit"). At around **6 months**, gently switch from alfalfa hay to **grass
hay** and reduce the pellets.

## Warning signs

In a young rabbit, see a rabbit-savvy vet **quickly** if you notice:

- **diarrhoea** or soft droppings that persist;
- a rabbit that **stops eating** or stops producing droppings;
- a **bloated belly**, a **hunched** or listless rabbit.

These are emergencies: in a baby rabbit, a gut that stops must be treated **without
delay**.
